DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Structure Born in Rain, Elevated Through Quiet Luxury.

Seattle is defined by its elemental contrast: harsh coastal downpours meeting soft, moody fog; industrial port steel softened by cedar forests and brutalist concrete.

Farhang was established to bridge this duality. We construct garments that withstand the elements while maintaining an uncompromising, architectural silhouette. Every piece uses custom-milled 380gsm–450gsm organic cottons, recycled wools, and Japanese selvedge denim.

"We don’t compete with fast fashion. We honor the ritual of dressing."

We rejected the flimsy, paper-thin fabrics of mass production in favor of heirloom textiles that gain character with every rainfall and every worn season.

GARMENT LONGEVITY & CARE GUIDE

Wash inside out on cold cycle (30°C) with mild eco-detergent. Hang dry away from direct sunlight to preserve deep organic dye richness and prevent shrinkage.

Wear continuously for 6 months before first wash to establish personal honeycomb creases. Spot clean with damp cloth when needed. When washing, submerge in cold water with ocean salt.

Professional eco-dry clean only. Use cedar hangers in cool, dark closets to maintain structural shoulder geometry and protect against moisture.
